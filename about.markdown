I'm **Gabriele Matini**, a Machine Learning and full-stack engineer. I like turning hard, ambiguous problems into systems that actually ship, with a bias toward **building robust and shipping fast**. Whether it's an AI feature, a web product, or the data and infrastructure underneath, I care about the same thing: software that holds up after launch, not just in a demo.

Most of my work lives at the intersection of AI and product engineering. I built and maintained the **RAG and ingestion systems at Sensay.io**, including a custom PDF chunking engine, and shipped features and support across their AI stack. For **xNovo AI** I created **Vantiq**, an agentic SEO-automation tool that clusters brand data, generates AI insights and seasonality forecasts, and performs semantic brand expansion. I also build and sell my own software, like **GrassHopper**, an agentic pipeline that streamlines job search and applications.

My day-to-day spans models and agentic workflows, web products and APIs, and the data and cloud foundations that keep everything reliable as complexity grows.
